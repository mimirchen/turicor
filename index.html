01The problem问题

Every TEER decision is still made by eye.TEER 的每一个决策，仍然靠肉眼。

Where to place the clip, when to grasp, whether to release. The operator reads a grey, moving image, and there is not a single number on the screen. The result decides the patient's outcome, and the result depends on who operates. 夹子放在哪、何时抓、能不能释放。术者读的是一幅灰色的动态图像，屏幕上没有一个数字。结果决定患者的结局，而结果取决于谁在做。

2–4mm

disagreement between four experienced specialists on the same frame and the same landmark: the papillary muscle that decides clip position.四位资深专家在同一帧、同一解剖标志上的分歧。这个标志是决定夹子位置的乳头肌。

Turicor inter-observer studyTuricor 观察者间一致性研究

death or heart-failure hospitalisation by two years, when residual leak after the procedure is minimal (≤1+) versus severe (≥3+).术后残余反流轻微（≤1+）与严重（≥3+）的患者，两年内死亡或心衰住院的比例。

COAPT · Kar et al., Circulation 2021

optimal procedural success from an operator's first 25 cases to 50+. Most centres never reach fifty cases a year.术者前 25 例到 50 例以上的最佳手术成功率。多数中心一年做不到 50 例。

02AutoClip

A number where there was a guess.原来靠猜的地方，现在有一个数字。

AutoClip segments 15 structures on every frame of the live echo (leaflet scallops, grippers, insertion points, papillary muscles, the clip) and turns them into a measurement for each of the three decisions. Decision support, not decision replacement: the operator stays in control. AutoClip 在实时超声的每一帧上分割 15 个结构（瓣叶分区、抓钳、插入点、乳头肌、夹子），把它们变成三个决策各自的测量值。辅助决策，不替代决策：控制权始终在术者手里。

07Where this goes方向

The intelligence layer of cardiac medicine.心脏医学的智能层。

We start with the mitral clip. The goal is AI that understands the beating heart in real time, for every catheter procedure. The operating room comes first because it is where perception and action already meet, and every case closes the loop.我们从二尖瓣夹子开始。目标是能实时理解跳动心脏的 AI，服务每一台导管手术。先从手术室做起，因为那里感知与动作已经相遇，每一台手术都让闭环转一圈。

  • Now现在Mitral TEER二尖瓣 TEER · one workflow, validated at outside centres一个工作流，在外部中心验证
  • Next下一步Tricuspid TEER, TAVI三尖瓣 TEER、TAVI · one shared heart model across procedures跨术式共用一个心脏模型
  • Later之后Beyond the valve瓣膜之外 · only where the evidence carries over只在证据能迁移的地方
