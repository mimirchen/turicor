In transcatheter edge-to-edge repair, the operator works entirely through a live ultrasound image. AutoClip reads that image, maps the valve and the clip on every frame, and gives the operator a number for each decision. 经导管缘对缘修复（TEER）里，术者完全依靠一幅实时超声图像操作。AutoClip 读懂这幅图像，在每一帧上画出瓣膜与夹子，并为每一个决策给出一个数字。

The problem问题

Every TEER decision is made by eye.TEER 的每一个决策都靠肉眼。

Where to clip, how deep to grasp, when to release: on today's screen there is not a single number. Give the same frame to four specialists and you get four different answers. That is why the learning curve is long, why results vary between operators, and why most hospitals cannot start. 夹在哪里、抓多深、何时释放：今天的屏幕上没有一个数字。同一帧给四位专家，得到四个答案。这就是学习曲线长、术者之间结果不一、多数医院开展不了的原因。

Fifteen structures on every frame of the live TEE: leaflet scallops, grippers, insertion points, papillary muscles, and the clip itself. Nothing changes in the workflow; it reads the ultrasound video output.在实时 TEE 的每一帧上分割十五个结构：瓣叶分区、抓钳、插入点、乳头肌和夹子本身。工作流不变，只接超声的视频输出。

Next

Navigate导航

A TEER procedure comes down to three decisions. The operator still decides. AutoClip gives a number for each.一台 TEER 归结为三个决策。决定权仍在术者，AutoClip 为每个决策给出一个数字。

  • AIs the clip in the right place?夹子位置对吗？Position on the papillary-muscle axis乳头肌轴上的内外侧位置
  • BShould the grippers drop?该放抓钳了吗？Insertion of both leaflets两侧瓣叶的插入
  • CShould we release?可以释放了吗？Grasped leaflet length, both sides两侧夹持瓣叶长度
North star

Discover发现

Every new readout is a candidate biomarker. An outcome-linked registry tests which of them predicts the result a year later. Biomarkers that predict become thresholds; thresholds become guideline language.每一个新读数都是候选生物标志物。结局关联登记库检验哪些能预测一年后的结果。能预测的成为阈值，阈值写进指南。
